Privacy

Line Clash · last updated 12 November 2026

Line Clash is a free browser game. This page explains what data the game uses and why. In short: you can play without an account, we don't ask for your email address, and we don't sell your data.

What the game stores

  • A random player code in your browser (local storage). It links your level, points and daily missions to you, without an account.
  • Your nickname, level and match results on our server, so they can appear on the leaderboards. Nicknames that are offensive are replaced.
  • Your settings (colour, trail, skills, sound) in your browser only.
  • Usage numbers: how many players and matches there are per day, to improve the game. For this we count player codes per day; these lists are kept for 60 days.

Players who haven't played for 60 days (with only a few matches) are removed automatically.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, so your level follows you to other devices, we receive a Google account id and your first name. We store only the account id, linked to your player code. We don't store your email address, and we never see your password.

Advertising

The game may show ads from Google (AdSense) on the screen before a match starts. Google and its partners can use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Visitors in the EU/EEA and UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

When you play Line Clash on CrazyGames, there are no Google sign-in or AdSense ads from us: ads and accounts there are handled by CrazyGames under their own privacy policy. We only receive your CrazyGames username, to show your name in the game.

Your choices

  • Clearing your browser's site data removes your player code and settings from that device.
  • To have your player data removed from our server, contact us (below) with your nickname.

Contact
